1. EXECUTIVE PRODUCT OVERVIEW & BRAND IDENTITY
================================================================================
Daymark reimagines the morning news routine as a finite, elegant, high-signal
digital broadsheet. Instead of endless algorithmic dopamine feeds, Daymark delivers
a personalized morning newspaper curated to the reader's intellectual lenses,
complete with an executive 3-takeaway intelligence box, lead cover story, audio
dispatch, and a deliberate "Outside Your Bubble" blindspot section to prevent
echo chambers.

- Product Name: Daymark
- Tagline: "Your world, clearly delivered."
- Visual Aesthetic: Premium editorial broadsheet inspired by Tide, Reflecta, and
  classic newsprint. Features warm ivory backgrounds (#F7F5EF), deep ink charcoal
  typography (#20211F), forest green accents (#496653), serif display titles,
  narrative drop caps, and authentic multi-column newspaper layouts.
- Core Value: Finite reading experience (5, 15, or 30 minutes), transparent
  explainable algorithmic ranking, zero ads, zero tracking, and zero subscription costs.

2. ZERO-BUDGET ARCHITECTURAL COMPLIANCE (PKR 0 TOTAL BUDGET)
================================================================================
Every subsystem was designed to strictly operate at $0.00 cost with no credit card:

1. News Ingestion: 100% Free Public RSS Feeds (BBC News, The New York Times,
   Ars Technica, NPR News). Fast parallel server-side fetch with 5-minute caching
   (next: { revalidate: 300 }) and 6-second timeout abort controllers.
2. AI / Executive Synthesis:
   - Free Google Gemini 1.5 Flash via REST API (using free Google AI Studio keys).
   - Deterministic Fallback Engine: If no key is provided or quota is exceeded,
     all summaries, takeaways, and explanations run locally with zero latency.
3. Audio Dispatch Engine: Native Browser Web Speech API (SpeechSynthesis). 100%
   free, client-side, zero audio synthesis token limits or costs.
4. Database & Storage:
   - Server-Side: Atomic file-backed JSON database at `data/daymark_db.json` (`lib/db.ts`).
   - Client-Side: Hydrated `localStorage` with cookie tokens for Next.js SSR middleware.
5. PDF Broadsheet Export: Standardized browser `@media print` CSS engine in
   `globals.css` formatted as a multi-column broadsheet.
6. Hosting: Vercel Hobby Tier (or Render/Netlify free tiers) with zero operational cost.

4. CORE ENGINE IMPLEMENTATIONS & ALGORITHMIC LOGIC
================================================================================
1. Transparent Ranking Engine (`lib/ranking.ts`):
   Every article is evaluated against a transparent composite score (0-100+ pts):
   - Topic Score (Base 0-30 pts): Matches reader's selected desks (+20 pts).
   - Topic Affinity Boost/Penalty (-12.5 to +12.5 pts): Calculated as `affinity * 2.5`
     based on reader's upvotes and downvotes.
   - Custom Keyword Match (0-35 pts): Substring search in title (+15 pts) and
     description (+8 pts) for each calibrated custom interest keyword.
   - Recency Score (0-20 pts): Linear decay over 48 hours (`(1 - ageHours / 48) * 20`).
   - Reading Pace Alignment (0-15 pts): Evaluates article word count against
     reader's pace (Quick: <500 words, Standard: 500-1200 words, Deep: >1200 words).
   - Negative Feedback Penalties:
     * Downvoted topics receive progressive suppression.
     * Skipped articles are completely excluded from the edition.

2. Anti-Echo-Chamber Blindspot Engine ("Outside Your Bubble"):
   - Identifies high-quality stories from categories outside the reader's selected topics.
   - Selects the highest-scoring unselected article and positions it with a distinct
     intellectual challenge badge.

3. Audio Dispatch Engine (`lib/audio.ts`):
   - Formats a continuous morning script: Masthead intro -> 3 Key Takeaways ->
     Cover Story -> Wire Headlines.
   - Uses client-side `window.speechSynthesis` with speech rate and pitch calibration.
   - Zero network latency, zero token costs.
